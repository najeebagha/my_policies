Privacy Policy for Auto Cricket Scorer
Effective Date: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how "Auto Cricket Scorer" (hereinafter referred to as "the App"), developed by Najeebullah, handles your information when you use our cricket scoring and team management application.

1. Strictly No Data Collection (Zero Data Harvesting)
The App is designed with a strict offline-first approach to respect your privacy. We do not collect, transmit, harvest, or store any of your personal data, match data, or files on our servers. Whether visible or hidden, no data is sent to us. Everything you create, score, or import stays 100% locally on your device.

2. Local Storage, Camera, and Gallery Access
To provide core functionalities, the App requires access to your device's camera and storage:

Player & Logos (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES): The App requests the READ_MEDIA_IMAGES permission to allow you to quickly batch-select player photos, team logos, and TV channel watermarks from your gallery. This ensures a seamless, uninterrupted live scoring and broadcasting experience without repeatedly navigating away to the system picker. These images are strictly saved locally in the App's database and are never uploaded to the internet.

Backup and Restore (.zkb files): The App uses the system File Picker to let you safely export (save) or import (restore) your complete database backups as .zkb files. We do not have access to these files; they are managed entirely by you on your local device storage.

3. Data Sharing (Manual Action Only)
The App provides features to share your match summaries, images, PDFs, and full database backups (.zkb files).

Manual Sharing: You can share this data via Bluetooth, WhatsApp, or other social apps. This sharing process is entirely manual and initiated by you. The App never automatically shares your files or data with anyone.

4. Google Ads and Internet Permission
The App is supported by advertisements.

Google AdMob: We have integrated Google AdMob to display ads. The App requires the Internet Permission solely to request and load these advertisements from Google’s ad network. Google may use advertising IDs to serve personalized ads based on their own privacy policies.

5. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions
The App offers an "Ad-Free" subscription service, providing options for both Monthly and Yearly durations to remove advertisements.

All transactions, renewals, and in-app purchases are processed securely through the Google Play Billing system.

We do not collect, process, or have access to any of your financial information, such as credit card numbers or bank details.

6. Local Server and Foreground Service
To broadcast live scores to OBS or Prism Live Studio, the App creates a Local HTTP Server via your local hotspot.

Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC): The App utilizes the FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC permission to ensure the local server remains active, syncs broadcast data to your local network, and manages database backups without disconnecting when the App is minimized.

Local Network Only: All data transmission for the live overlay happens strictly within your Local Area Network (LAN). No score data is transmitted to the wider internet or external servers.

7. Required Permissions Summary

Camera: To capture live pictures for players or logos.

READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / Storage: To quickly access and load user-selected media assets (player photos, logos, watermarks) directly into the live broadcast, and handle .zkb backup files.

Internet / Network State: Required to load Google Ads and manage the local hotspot server connection.

FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC: To maintain the background live broadcast server and process local data syncing tasks.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the App or on our official website. We advise you to review this page periodically.
